# 05 — Behavioral & Leadership

## Story bank

Build 8–10 reusable stories around:
- architecture disagreement
- influence without authority
- failure
- production incident
- ambiguity
- migration
- mentoring
- cross-team delivery
- raising standards
- strategic investment

## STAR structure

### Situation
Keep it short and establish stakes.

### Task
Explain what you personally owned.

### Action
Spend most of the answer here:
- decisions
- alternatives
- stakeholder management
- technical reasoning
- execution

### Result
Use concrete metrics where they are supported.

### Reflection
What changed in your engineering judgment afterward?

## Senior Staff emphasis

Interviewers should hear:
- "I created clarity."
- "I aligned teams."
- "I made a difficult trade-off."
- "I established a mechanism that scaled beyond me."
- "I can explain what I would do differently today."
